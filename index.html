Screen 7 of 9 · Block 5 · 12 min

Blockchain or database?

  • At work you will not build a blockchain. You will be asked to approve one.
  • Use five questions on three real proposals.

The five questions

  1. Do several organisations need to write to the same record?
  2. Is there a party they all trust that can keep it? If yes, that party runs a database.
  3. Can every entry be permanent and visible to all participants?
  4. Does the asset live on the ledger or outside it? If outside, who guarantees the link between the two?
  5. Who holds the keys, and who pays to run the network?
