            Screen 7 of 8 · Block 5 · 12 min

            Blockchain or database?

            The story

            • You now know the four mechanisms and what each one costs.
            • At work you will not build a blockchain. You will be asked to approve one.
            • Use the five questions on three real proposals.

            Your task · 4 min

            1. Read the five questions.
            2. Read each proposal and vote.
            3. Reveal what happened.

            The five-question test

            1. Do several organisations need to write to the same record?
            2. Is there a party they all trust that can keep it? If yes, that party runs a database.
            3. Can every entry be permanent and visible to all participants?
            4. Does the asset live on the ledger or outside it? If outside, who guarantees the link between the two?
            5. Who holds the keys, and who pays to run the network?

            Questions 1 to 3 are adapted from Wüst and Gervais, "Do you need a Blockchain?". Questions 4 and 5 come from Blocks 1 to 3.

            Vote on all three proposals first.

            Takeaway

            A blockchain is justified only when several parties must write to one record and none of them is trusted to keep it. Otherwise a database is cheaper.
